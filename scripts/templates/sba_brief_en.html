03 / dates before models

Let outcomes mature
before the next stage.

  1. Train: 20,000 loans funded in 2006–2010. Fit preprocessing and Logistic only on training loans. The latest 24-month horizon ends in 2012.
  2. Calibrate separately: 5,000 loans funded in 2013. Only these labels fit isotonic calibration. Their latest horizon ends in 2015.
  3. Test: 10,000 loans funded in 2016–2017. Evaluate fixed models and capacities. Test outcomes do not tune features or hyperparameters.

The final public snapshot has actual funding dates but no historical first-publication times or feature revisions. This is retrospective funding-cohort validation. It cannot certify which features were available at past decisions or directly represent current Chinese retail credit.
