The contract between the two systems

Decision dates mean UTC end of day. Availability is the later of publication and ingestion. Observations older than seven days, deleted records and missing features are rejected. Utilization and debt-to-income are fractions; prior delinquencies is a count. Principal remains integer USD cents.

This example includes historical corrections published on the decision date but ingested the next day. They must not replace the selected earlier record. Public inputs are synthetic. The 180-day 90+ DPD label is a research definition; calibrated probabilities and model scores are not a deployed lending policy.
