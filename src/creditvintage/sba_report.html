A frozen funding-cohort study of 35,000 US small-business loans. Learn on earlier loans, calibrate later, then evaluate on 2016–2017 funding cohorts.

Retrospective validation, with explicit limits. The official final snapshot contains funding and outcome dates, but no first-publication times or historical feature revisions. This study cannot certify what was publicly available at each past decision. It does not invent available_at timestamps.

01 / dates before models

Three cohorts. Mature outcomes between stages.

The 24-month outcome window ends before the next stage starts. One earliest eligible loan per hashed recorded borrower group; no group appears twice. Fixed sizes and hash-based sampling do not balance outcomes.

Wide tables scroll horizontally on small screens. Group keys use normalized recorded name/state/ZIP. Spelling and address changes can evade this heuristic. Large initial-rate missingness in earlier cohorts is retained and disclosed.

02 / fixed protocol, retained results

Calibration and ranking answer different questions.

Constant train rate, logistic regression and separate isotonic calibration use the same test loans. Lower Brier is better; higher AUC and average precision are better. Calibration can improve Brier while reducing ranking performance.

@@METRICS@@
MethodROC AUCAverage precisionBrier score

95% percentile intervals: 300 paired resamples of the 24 test funding months. Models remain fixed. Intervals exclude source revision, training, parameter and sample-selection uncertainty; one-class replicates remain in the evidence with unavailable metrics and valid counts.

What the model actually sees

Log approval amount, SBA guaranteed share, initial interest rate and approval processing method. Median imputation with missing indicators, scaling and category encoding are learned from training loans only. No dates, cohort IDs, borrower identities, final status, current term or outcome fields enter the model.

03 / comparable workload

Hold the review budget fixed.

Each method ranks the same 10,000 test loans; ties use record ID. Captured events describe historical concentration. They do not measure prevented loss, approval policy, recoveries or causal benefit.

05 / bring the evidence with you

Run, refit, and check offline.

The installed wheel contains the frozen source, protocol and conversion audit. Running the study needs no source checkout, raw 574 MB download or optional PITBridge dependency.

python -m creditvintage sba-benchmark --out outputs/sba-credit
python -m creditvintage verify-sba outputs/sba-credit

The verifier checks source and protocol, refits all three methods, rebuilds metrics, paired bootstrap, review order and HTML, then compares the portable ZIP. Rehashing fabricated metrics does not make them valid.
